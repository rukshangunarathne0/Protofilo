# ◈ WHOLESALE DOCTRINE

> Why this system exists and the principles it must never violate.

## CHARTER

Happy Time sells watches and clocks wholesale. **Happy Time Connect** exists so a dealer and a staff member see the same truth at the same moment — no double-selling, no lost order, no phone call that ends in "I will check and tell you."

## THE FOUR PROMISES

1. **The number on screen is the number on the shelf.** → [[07 INVENTORY CORE]]
2. **An order, once placed, is never lost.** → [[06 ORDER PIPELINE]]
3. **A dealer sees only their own business.** → [[09 DEALER REGISTRY]]
4. **A promise of future stock is tracked, not remembered.** → [[08 PREORDER TRACKER]]

## THE THREE FAILURES TO AVOID

| FAILURE | COST | GUARD |
|---|---|---|
| Overselling | reputation, refunds | reservation discipline |
| Untrustworthy totals | disputes, unpaid invoices | computed money only |
| Open data | the business itself | [[05 SECURITY PERIMETER]] |

## SERVICE STANDARD

- Answer a dealer within one working day → [[12 STAFF RUNBOOK]]
- Support line **077 352 8760**, call or WhatsApp → [[10 FEEDBACK SIGNAL]]
- Pre-order dates always updated, never silent → [[08 PREORDER TRACKER]]

## GROWTH ORDER

Fix the perimeter → earn real identity → trust the numbers → then grow the network. Growth on a broken foundation multiplies the damage. → [[16 ROADMAP PHASES]]
