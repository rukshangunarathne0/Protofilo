# Short-video concepts

Both concepts are designed for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok, and WhatsApp Status. Silent MP4 edits are included in `assets/videos/`; add platform-cleared music or an approved English/Sinhala voiceover before publishing. Use real product images from the catalogue; do not add unverified specifications or artificial stock claims.

## Concept 01 — The Detail Reveal

**Purpose:** turn the Hublot skeleton dial into a close-up, saveable product reveal.  
**Length:** 10–12 seconds  
**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 24 or 30 fps  
**Product:** `RSD-WS-143` — Hublot Round Skeleton Chronograph Watch  
**Tone:** precise, dark, quietly premium

### Timeline

| Time | Visual / movement | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8s | Black screen. A thin gold rule draws from left to right. | `LOOK CLOSER.` / `සමීපව බලන්න.` |
| 0.8–3.3s | Slow push-in on the full watch image. Keep the dial in the centre safe area. | `MECHANICAL, IN FULL VIEW.` |
| 3.3–6.2s | Crop closer to the blue skeleton dial; use a gentle left-to-right pan. | `SKELETON DETAIL` / `යාන්ත්‍රික හැඩය` |
| 6.2–8.8s | Pull back to the full watch and box. | `LIMITED AVAILABILITY` |
| 8.8–11.5s | R.S.D. mark, gold rule, WhatsApp CTA. | `ORDER VIA WHATSAPP 0711368623` / `WhatsApp මගින් ඇණවුම් කරන්න` |

### Edit direction

- Use a slow 103% to 112% scale animation rather than a fast spin or fake mechanical movement.
- Keep all text inside the centre 80% safe zone so TikTok/Reels UI does not cover it.
- Use hard cuts or 6-frame cross-dissolves; avoid generic flashy transitions.
- Grade to the existing R.S.D. palette: near-black, warm white, muted gold, and the watch’s blue.
- Audio: low-tempo instrumental with a subtle tick or click texture. Use a licensed track or platform-cleared sound.
- Optional voiceover: English or Sinhala should be recorded separately after the timing is approved.

## Concept 02 — Choose Your Mood

**Purpose:** make the catalogue feel personal and encourage comments/saves.  
**Length:** 12–15 seconds  
**Format:** 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 24 or 30 fps  
**Products:** Hublot `RSD-WS-143`, Tissot `RSD-WS-165`, Chanel `RSD-WS-147`  
**Tone:** editorial, clean, comment-friendly

### Timeline

| Time | Visual / movement | On-screen copy |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.0s | R.S.D. logo on dark background. | `CHOOSE YOUR MOOD.` / `ඔබේ mood එක තෝරන්න.` |
| 1.0–4.2s | Hublot image with a slow upward move. | `THE STATEMENT` / `ප්‍රකාශය` |
| 4.2–7.4s | Tissot turquoise image with a gentle rightward move. | `THE COLOUR` / `වර්ණය` |
| 7.4–10.6s | Chanel silver image with a subtle pull-back. | `THE SILHOUETTE` / `හැඩය` |
| 10.6–14.5s | Three slim image strips or a clean three-word end card. | `WHICH ONE IS YOURS?` / `ඔබේ තේරීම කුමක්ද?` |

### Edit direction

- Cut on the beat, but let each product breathe for at least three seconds.
- Use one consistent gold label position across all three product frames.
- Do not add prices unless the current catalogue price and availability are checked immediately before publishing.
- End with a comment prompt first, then the WhatsApp CTA in the caption; this makes the video feel less like an advert.
- Audio: one restrained, licensed instrumental track. No unlicensed luxury-brand audio or copied campaign music.
