# Summary

## What is this project?

This is the **Happy Time Web App**.

## Who is it for?

It is made for our **wholesale customers**.

## Why do we have it?

The app is built to make work **easy for our online staff**. Instead of doing everything by hand or in different places, our staff can use one simple app to handle their daily work faster and with fewer mistakes.

## In simple words

Happy Time is a web app where our wholesale customers are served, and our online staff can do their work easily in one place.
